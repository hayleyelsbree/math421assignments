17. Write a paragraph to summarize the section and give your comments on the results. You could do some other calculations to support your points.

There are many findings from the hospitalization dataset found from this data wrangling. For example, we got a better idea of the scope of the dataset. For example, we learned that there are more females patients in the dataset than males, but that males are of higher age than females. Additionally, October seems to be the most frequent month for being admitted, because it is the month with the most patients admitted overall as well as by male only. The average total cost of a hospital visit at a provider is at most $69,945.55 at Bradley, but the most common provider is not Bradley but rather Rhode Island Hospital (see below code chunk). Based on the seasonality analysis, summer and fall are roughly the same in terms of average length of a stay, however there is not much variation by season, demonstrating that season might not influence the length of stay for a patient as much as another variable might. There is still much more analysis that could be done, which will be explored in the Data Visualization section of the project.
