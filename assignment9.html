The top 3 countries with the most cumulative deaths are the US, Brazil, and India.

  1. Use transition_reveal to make a plot revealing the number of deaths in the three countries with the most numbers of deaths
library(gganimate)
## Warning: package 'gganimate' was built under R version 4.4.3
df |> 
  filter(Country_code %in% c("US", "BR", "IN")) |> 
  ggplot(aes(y = Cumulative_deaths, x = Date_reported, color = Country)) +
  geom_line() +
  transition_reveal(Date_reported) +
  labs(x='Date Reported', 
       y = 'Cumulative Deaths', 
       color='Country', 
       title = 'Cumulative Deaths in the US, Brazil, and India Over Time', 
       caption = 'The US has more cumulative deaths than Brazil and India consistently over time.')
